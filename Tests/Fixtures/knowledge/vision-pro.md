# Apple Vision Pro 与空间计算

Apple 在 2024 年 2 月发售了 Vision Pro 头显。它运行 visionOS，依靠眼动追踪、手势和语音输入，没有手柄。

## Key facts

- Price: $3,499 in the US at launch.
- Chips: M2 for compute plus the R1 sensor processor (12 ms photon-to-photon latency).
- Displays: two micro-OLED panels, about 23 million pixels in total.
- Sam Altman said spatial computing is "still early" when asked about the headset.

## Why it matters

The device shows what a computer without a screen on a desk could feel like: windows float in the room, and the notch disappears entirely.
